I'm an IB Diploma student at Dhirubhai Ambani International School, Mumbai. I'm interested in how human cognition, logic, language and culture shape the way we reason, and in how far AI can represent and replicate those processes.

I come at these questions from several directions: documenting how languages mix on the streets of Baku, using Carnatic music to help teenagers who stutter, measuring how a chatbot conversation changes adolescents' moral decisions, building tools that catch flawed arguments, and proving results about the data that learning algorithms collect. I want to keep learning across linguistics, psychology, philosophy, mathematics and computer science, because the questions I care about don't belong to any one of them.
